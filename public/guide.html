COINNEST GUIDE

Your coins, explained.

Understand your balance, optional Android videos and redemption history before choosing a reward.

Start with your account

Create an account using your name, email address and a password of at least ten characters. Use a unique password and sign out on shared devices. CoinNest stores your coin balance on its server, so refreshing the page does not reset your balance.

The website keeps an authenticated browser session for up to thirty days. If a session expires, sign in again to access your balance and activity. Never share your password with someone claiming they can increase your coins.

How optional video rewards work

The Android app offers optional rewarded videos. The confirmation explains the reward before a video starts. You can decline and keep using your account. Test advertisements never add production coins.

A completed live video is checked through Google's server-side verification callback before coins are credited. Closing a video early does not qualify. If verification takes time, check Activity later. Repeatedly tapping the video button will not speed up verification.

Standard website display advertisements are separate: viewing or clicking these advertisements does not earn coins. Only select an advertisement if its content interests you.

Choose a reward carefully

Open Rewards to review each item's title, coin cost and availability. A disabled item is unavailable. Confirm a redemption only after checking the cost. An eligible redemption subtracts coins from your account and creates a record visible in Activity.

CoinNest points have no cash value. External game top-ups are unavailable. Your reward records are linked to your account. Do not assume that an unavailable catalog entry can be purchased or transferred.

Read your activity

Coin history lists credits and deductions with their timestamps. Redemptions lists the reward and recorded status. Compare these records with your current balance when checking a completed video or redemption. Refresh once to retrieve current server data.

If the server cannot be reached, keep your account details private and try again when your connection is stable. A display error alone does not prove that a reward was credited. The server record determines your balance.

Advertisements and privacy

If enabled, this guide can display Google advertisements in clearly labelled sections between or after its content. Google may use cookies or device information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Read Google's advertising information for details.

Advertising is optional on this page and stays disabled until you choose to load it. Reloading the page resets that choice. You can use the complete guide without advertisements. Android ad privacy options are available inside the app.
